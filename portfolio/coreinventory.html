The Challenge

CoreInventory, an early-stage startup, had built a powerful inventory management SaaS product but lacked a professional web presence to attract B2B customers. Their existing landing page was a single-page template that didn't communicate the product's value proposition or generate qualified leads.

They needed a website that would showcase product features, build credibility with enterprise buyers, and create a demo request pipeline.

Our Solution

We designed a comprehensive product marketing website that positions CoreInventory as a trusted enterprise solution.

  • Dashboard-style hero section with animated product screenshots
  • Feature pages with interactive demos and comparison tables
  • Tiered pricing page with clear CTAs for each plan
  • Case study section showcasing existing customer success stories
  • Lead capture forms with email automation integration
  • Blog section for SEO and thought leadership content
  • Performance-optimized for fast loading and SEO rankings

The Results

The new website became CoreInventory's primary lead generation channel, consistently driving qualified demo requests from target customers.
