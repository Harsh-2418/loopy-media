The Challenge

Diamanik Hospital Consultancy had zero online presence. Potential clients searching for hospital management services in Ahmedabad couldn't find them. Competitors with professional websites were capturing all the leads, while Diamanik relied entirely on word-of-mouth referrals — which limited their growth potential significantly.

They needed a website that would establish credibility, showcase their consultancy services, and generate a steady stream of qualified leads from Google search.

Our Solution

We designed and developed a complete digital presence for Diamanik Hospital Consultancy, focusing on trust, professionalism, and lead generation.

  • Professional multi-page website with clean medical aesthetic
  • Service pages for each consultancy offering with clear CTAs
  • Team profiles showcasing expertise and credentials
  • WhatsApp and phone integration for instant inquiries
  • Google My Business optimization for local search visibility
  • On-page SEO targeting "hospital consultant Ahmedabad" keywords
  • Mobile-responsive design for patients browsing on phones
